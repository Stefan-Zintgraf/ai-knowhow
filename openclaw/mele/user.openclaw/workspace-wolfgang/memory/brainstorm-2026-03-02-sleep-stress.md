# BMAD Brainstorm Session — Mental Health (Sleep & Stress)
**Date:** 2026-03-02  
**Topic:** Reducing stress and improving sleep quality (middle-of-night wakeups)

---

## Original Problem
- Too stressed overall
- Difficulty sleeping specifically: **falling asleep is fine, but waking up in the night with racing thoughts**
- Brain uses 3am to solve all life's problems

---

## Idea Clusters Generated

1. **Wind-down rituals** — signals to brain that it's safe to shut down
2. **Morning anchors** — starting the day right reduces evening anxiety
3. **Stress "containers"** — limiting when/where you worry
4. **Environmental tweaks** — small physical changes with big impact
5. **Digital boundaries** — reducing the stimulation firehose
6. **Micro-recovery** — brief resets during the day
7. **Cognitive offloading** — getting worries out of your head

---

## Top 3 General Strategies (Initial)

### 1. The 60-Minute Wind-Down (Ritual)
No screens, dim lights, same 2-3 calming activities 60 min before bed.
- *Pivot note: Less relevant since falling asleep isn't the issue*

### 2. The "Worry Window" (Stress Container)
Schedule 15-20 min in late afternoon/evening to write down all stressors.

### 3. Phone Bedroom Ban
Charge phone outside bedroom, use real alarm clock.
- *Pivot note: May help prevent initial wakeups from notifications*

---

## Targeted Strategies for Middle-of-Night Wakeups

### 1. The "Brain Dump" Notebook (by the bed)
Pen and paper within arm's reach. Write down thoughts without solving them.
→ *Satisfies brain's need to "not forget" so it can let go*

### 2. The "Paradoxical Intention" Trick
Instead of trying to sleep, tell yourself "I'm just going to rest here." Removes pressure.

### 3. The "Quarter-Hour Rule"
If awake ~15 min with racing mind, get up. Dim light, boring activity (book, NOT phone), return when sleepy.
→ *Breaks "bed = worrying" association*

### 4. Pre-emptive Worry Window (shifted earlier)
Move worry time to late evening (~9pm) to process concerns BEFORE sleep.

### 5. Temperature Check
Cool room (16-19°C) + warm feet (socks). Core temp drop signals sleepiness.

---

## Quick-Test Protocol (Suggested)
1. Put notebook + pen on nightstand now
2. When waking up → write down the thought
3. Tell yourself: "Noted. Morning-me will handle this."
4. If still awake 15 min later → get up, dim light, boring book, return when sleepy
