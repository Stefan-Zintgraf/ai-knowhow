Hi,<div><br></div><div>yes you need a ProgId for example. We use Wix Setup for EC-Engineer. There it looks like this:</div><div><br></div><div>
<div>&lt;ProgId Id="ECEngineerProjectFile" Icon="ECEngineerIco" Description="EC-Engineer Project File"&gt;</div>
<div>          &lt;Extension Id="ecc" &gt;</div>
<div>            &lt;Verb Id="open" Command="Open" TargetFile="ECEngineer" Argument="/CFG=&amp;quot;%1&amp;quot;" /&gt;</div>
<div>          &lt;/Extension&gt;</div>
<div><span>&lt;/ProgId&gt;</span></div>
<div><span><br></span></div>
<div><span>There is also the CommandLine part. </span></div>
<div><span><br></span></div>
<div><span>Best regards,</span></div>
