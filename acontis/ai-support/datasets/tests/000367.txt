<div>Hi Justin,</div><div><br></div><div>the notification mechanism should not be called in your application. During the call to our <span>ecatExecJob(), the callback function passed to </span><span>ecatRegisterClient() will be called and notify your application that the transfer has been done successfully or failed.</span>
</div><div><span><br></span></div><div><span>The following code is wrong and should not exist:</span></div><div>
<div><br></div>
<div>// Notification (Steps 10-11; having some issue here)</div>
<div>	CEmNotification obj(dwMasterInstance);</div>
<div>	EC_T_DWORD dwCode = EC_NOTIFY_MBOXRCV;</div>
<div>	EC_T_NOTIFYPARMS* pParms;</div>
<div>	pParms-&gt;pbyInBuf = pMbxTfer;</div>
<div>	pParms-&gt;dwInBufSize = 1;</div>
<div>	obj.ecatNotify(dwCode,pParms);</div>
