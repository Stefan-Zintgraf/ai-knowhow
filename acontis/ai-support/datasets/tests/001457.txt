### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
EtherCAT Master Programming Interface Inquiry

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div dir="ltr">
<div>Dear Sir/Madam,</div>
<div> </div>
<div>We are a foundry plant and machine manufacturer located in Tak, Thailand. Our products include sand casting parts and also offer automation for general machinery with the expertise focused in the foundry industry.<br>
</div>
<div> </div>
<div>Our current project is focused on foundry purposed robotics with the following architecture:</div>
<div>1) Kinematic solver, trajectory generation, main controller and user interface(all written in C++ and Python) on a Linux PC with PREEMPTIVE_RT patch<br>
</div>
<div>2) EtherCAT servo drives (Yaskawa Sigma 7, and/or Delta ASDA A2)<br>
</div>
<div>3) EtherCAT slave station (Digital Inputs and Outputs)</div>
<div> </div>
<div>As the developers are closing in to the integration stage, we require a EtherCAT master solution for the Linux PC to connect with the EtherCAT slave devices. From the documentations found, acontis technology EC-Master is suitable for various operating systems such as Windows, Linux and many other various OS.<br>
</div>
<div>However upon closer inspection, it appears that the EC-Master can even be used to integrate into a full fledged general purpose controller( for example PLC). This is quite excessive for our current application.<br>
</div>
<div>In order to speed up the developing process, we are wondering if acontis also offers:</div>
<div>1) C++ motion control library similar to that of IEC-61131 PLCopen. (MC_Power, MC_MoveAbsolute(continous update), MC_GearIn...).<br>
</div>
<div>2) C++ interface to read and write simple digital input/outputs from/to the EtherCAT slave station.<br>
</div>
<div> </div>
<div>Best Regards,</div>
<div>Derek Chiu</div>
<div>Mechatronic Engineer</div>
<div>Maesot Heavy Industry<br>
</div>
</div>
