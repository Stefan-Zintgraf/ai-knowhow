<div>Thanks for reaching out! Our project involves a laser scanner mounted on a motor for high-precision underwater scanning. An important requirement is to get the motor's position with minimal latency, particularly while it is spinning during a scan. Our previous communication protocol, RS-485, had a round-trip time of approximately 9ms, which was too high. We were then told to explore EtherCAT instead.<br><br>Our setup is as follows:<br><ul>
<li>Master Controller: NVIDIA Jetson Xavier running Ubuntu 18.04</li>
<li>EtherCAT Slave: Oriental Motor AZD-KRED EtherCAT driver (we have an ESI file)</li>
<li>Language: C/C++</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div>As a part of our initial investigation into EtherCAT Masters, we investigated SOEM and IgH EtherCAT Master. We are now investigating commercial solutions like acontis EC-Master on Linux. Our requirements are mostly around querying the motor position, moving to specific positions, and getting positions while spinning.<br><br>We were hoping to request the following:<br><ul>
<li>An evaluation/demo of acontis' products, whatever would work best for our Linux-based platforms</li>
<li>Information regarding costs associated with using acontis, such as licensing, and anything else relevant</li>
