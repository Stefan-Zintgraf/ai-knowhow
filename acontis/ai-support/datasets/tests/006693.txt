### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
Blank FoE filenames

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>At the 2023 EU Plug Fest, I had a confusing error message when I left the filename blank in the FoE upload/download. I later did some reading, and in <b>ETG1000.6</b>, section 5.8.1 Read Request and 5.8.2 Write Request, I find that the Mailbox Header Length field (see images below) is specified as "N &gt; 0x06". This implies that the filename must be at least 1 character long.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Would it be prudent to detect this in the UI and reject it with a message something like “Slave filename must not be empty.”? Or have you found that some slaves require supporting a blank filename?</p>
