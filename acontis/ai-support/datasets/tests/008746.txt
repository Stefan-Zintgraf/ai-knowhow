Hi,<div><br></div><div>the ConfigData in the EEPROM section of the ESI of the slave tells us, that this slave has no MCU. Therefore it uses Device Emulation. With DeviceEmulation active we change the transition of the InitCommand to SO. So this seems to be a problem in the ESI. Old EC-Engineer did not check the DeviceEmulation.</div><div><br></div><div>Best regards,</div><div>Kai</div><div>
