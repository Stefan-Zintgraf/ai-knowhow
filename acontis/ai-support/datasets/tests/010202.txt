### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
Support Request: Configuration and Shutdown Processes for EcMaster

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>Hello! I work at Brooks Automation, and I have some questions regarding the configuration and shutdown processes for the EcMaster.<br>
<br> As part of our existing set up, we require the ability to reconfigure our robots with different settings without a power cycle. To achieve this, we reset each of our components to a "default" state and then reconfigure them with the new settings. As we integrate your SDK, we want to mirror this with the EcMaster for various settings including but not limited to loading a different ENI file. </div><div> </div><div>It is our current understanding that to do this we must first shutdown the master (pausing and deinitializing various components) and then reinitialize everything with the new settings. For this process, we referenced the "Exit" section of your MotionDemo as a guide for the order in which components need to be shut down.</div><div> </div><div>We have successfully seen this reconfiguration work in our environment; however, we are experiencing issues where, after a couple of reconfigurations, the software crashes. We currently hypothesize that it is related to a memory management issue and want to know if there are additional steps we should take in our "shutdown" with respect to the EcMaster. Your demo seems to show how to safely shut down the various components before ceasing operation, but we are unsure if this procedure accounts for memory management when initializing and configuring a new master within the same runtime session.</div><div> </div><div>Please let us know if you have any specific examples or suggestions for actions we should take to safely reconfigure with respect to the acontis SDK.</div><div> </div><div>Thank you for any advice you may have!</div><div> </div><div>

### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
Support Request: Configuration and Shutdown Processes for EcMaster

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi Samantha,<div><br></div><div>
<div>nice to hear from you!</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>
<div>In principle, multiple initialization and deinitialization with our EC-Master stack within a single process run should <b>not</b> be a problem.</div>
<div>In our unit tests as well as our functional tests, we do this many hundreds to thousands of times within the same process run. We also use <a href="https://valgrind.org/" title="https://valgrind.org/" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://valgrind.org/</a> to ensure we do our memory/resource management correctly.</div>
</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>
<div>Many of our function calls come in pairs, e.g. ecatInitMaster and ecatDeinitMaster or ecatRegisterClient and ecatUnregisterClient.</div>
<div><span>Personally, I would use the EcMasterDemo as a guide, as this is better maintained than the EcMasterDemoMotion.</span></div>
<div><br></div>
<div>In principle you should perform the following step with respect to the EC-Master:</div>
<div>
<ol>
<li>Bring the network to Init</li>
<li>Unregister your client</li>
<li>Stop the RAS server if it is running</li>
<li>Wait until the “JobTask” has finished ...</li>
<li>... and then deinitialize the EC-Master.</li>
</ol>
<div>See also Examples/ECMasterDemo/EcDemoApp.cpp <span>EC_T_DWORD EcDemoApp(T_EC_DEMO_APP_CONTEXT* pAppContext) at the very end of the function.</span>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div><span><br></span></div>
<div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
<div><span>Your application SW must be considered separately.</span></div>
<div><span><br></span></div>
<div>I have a feeling it might be a threading problem. That possibly the "JobTask" (the loop that calls continuously <span>ecatExecJob(...)</span><span>) (or another thread) is still running whether the EC-Master has already been deinitialized. Can you ensure the "JobTask" has ended and stopping it did not run in to time out or something. I would take a closer look at this. I don't think you can avoid debugging this.</span><br>
</div>
