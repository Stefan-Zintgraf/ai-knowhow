### ARTICLE02_BODY
Dear Cyril san

I apologize for asking you the same question over and over again.
They are considering ways to minimize the jitter in the PDO communication cycle.

1. Since having tasks split into two—TimingTask and ECMasterJobTask—increases overhead from the scheduler and dispatcher,
    They  would like to eliminate the TimingTask and modify the ECMasterJobTask to be triggered at specified intervals by the hardware timer interrupt handler.
    Is this change possible?

2. If the above change is possible, They  understand that we can achieve this by modifying the wai_flg in the OsWaitForEvent of the ECMasterJobTask from "set_flg from TimingTask" to "set_flg from the hardware timer interrupt handler.
    Is that correct?

3. If both points 1 and 2 are feasible, can we set the cycle of the hardware timer interrupt handler using the variable AppContext.AppParms.dwBusCycleTimeUsec from the sample code?

Best regards,
Takuya Fujikawa
