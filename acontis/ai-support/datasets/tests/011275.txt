Dear Mr. Fujikawa,<div><br></div><div>actually the EC-Master library is compiled with '-O3'. The only reason '-O0' switch is used for EcMasterDemo is debugging comfort: if you build your application with '-O3' you will probably get strange debugger behavior.</div><div>If you still want to switch the optimization on for your application change 'OPT += -O0' to i.e. 'OPT += -O3' in 'Workspace\uC3_iMX8\EcMasterDemo\config.mk' (line 10) and rebuild EcMasterDemo and EcMasterDemo_XXXX respectively. Please note: this will not affect optimization level of delivered 'libEcMaster.a' and 'libFslFec.a', they will be still '-O3' optimized.</div><div><br></div><div>
