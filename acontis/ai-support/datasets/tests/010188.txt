Hi Hannah,<div><br></div><div><div>unfortunately, you no longer have maintenance. Your maintenance contract expired on 28.02.2022. For further support, please contact our sales team at sales@acontis.com.</div></div><div><br></div><div>The EL6695 connects two EtherCAT networks. There is a primary and a secondary side. The primary side is addressed via E-BUS and the secondary side via RJ45 connector. <span>The secondary side requires a separate power supply. See also: </span><a href="https://download.beckhoff.com/download/Document/io/ethercat-terminals/el6695_en.pdf" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://download.beckhoff.com/download/Document/io/ethercat-terminals/el6695_en.pdf</a>. "<span>The power supply for the secondary side (RJ 45) is via an external
connection, the primary side is supplied via the E-bus. Thanks to the flexible CoE configuration, the bridge
terminal can also be used to integrate a subordinate IPC systems as an EtherCAT slave."</span>
</div><div><span><br></span></div><div><div>Normally you configure the device from the primary side, for which you will most likely need a bus coupler such as the EK1100.</div></div><div><div><a href="https://www.beckhoff.com/de-de/produkte/i-o/ethercat-klemmen/ek1xxx-bk1xx0-ethercat-koppler/" title="https://www.beckhoff.com/de-de/produkte/i-o/ethercat-klemmen/ek1xxx-bk1xx0-ethercat-koppler/" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://www.beckhoff.com/de-de/produkte/i-o/ethercat-klemmen/ek1xxx-bk1xx0-ethercat-koppler/</a></div></div><div><div><a href="https://www.beckhoff.com/de-de/produkte/i-o/ethercat-klemmen/ek1xxx-bk1xx0-ethercat-koppler/ek1100.html" title="https://www.beckhoff.com/de-de/produkte/i-o/ethercat-klemmen/ek1xxx-bk1xx0-ethercat-koppler/ek1100.html" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://www.beckhoff.com/de-de/produkte/i-o/ethercat-klemmen/ek1xxx-bk1xx0-ethercat-koppler/ek1100.html</a></div></div><div><br></div><div>Hope that helps so far.</div><div><br></div><div>Best regards,</div><div>
