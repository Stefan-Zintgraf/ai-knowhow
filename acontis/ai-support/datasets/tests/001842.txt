<p>I hope that you had a productive week and also enjoyed some experiences in Germany with your family. Hopefully all went well on your travels back to the US.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Our engineer Max was able to reproduce the issue you have with this. He suggests the below workaround for now to keep you moving forward until we complete the portation work and official support:</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It is possible to change the `LinkOsAllocDmaBuffer` function in `LinkOsLayer.cpp`</p>
<p>from</p>
<p>```</p>
<p>EC_FNNAME EC_T_VOID EC_FNCALL LinkOsAllocDmaBuffer(</p>
<p>EC_T_VOID* pvLinkOsContext,</p>
<p>EC_T_BYTE** pbyVirtAddrCached,</p>
<p>EC_T_BYTE** ppbyVirtAddrUncached,</p>
<p>EC_T_UINT64* pqwPhysAddr,</p>
<p>EC_T_DWORD dwSize)</p>
<p>{</p>
<p>ExtendedAddress dma_bus_addr;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>EC_UNREFPARM(pvLinkOsContext);</p>
<p>EC_UNREFPARM(dwSize);</p>
<p> </p>
<p>*pbyVirtAddrCached = (EC_T_BYTE*)S_dma_virt;</p>
<p>*ppbyVirtAddrUncached = (EC_T_BYTE*)S_dma_virt;</p>
<p>dma_bus_addr = *((ExtendedAddress*)(S_dma_virt + 0x100000 - sizeof(ExtendedAddress)));</p>
<p>*pqwPhysAddr = dma_bus_addr;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>return;</p>
<p>}</p>
<p>```</p>
<p>to</p>
<p>```</p>
<p>EC_FNNAME EC_T_VOID EC_FNCALL LinkOsAllocDmaBuffer(</p>
<p>EC_T_VOID* pvLinkOsContext,</p>
<p>EC_T_BYTE** pbyVirtAddrCached,</p>
<p>EC_T_BYTE** ppbyVirtAddrUncached,</p>
<p>EC_T_UINT64* pqwPhysAddr,</p>
<p>EC_T_DWORD dwSize)</p>
<p>{</p>
<p>ExtendedAddress dma_bus_addr;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>EC_UNREFPARM(pvLinkOsContext);</p>
<p>EC_UNREFPARM(dwSize);</p>
<p> </p>
<p>*pbyVirtAddrCached = (EC_T_BYTE*)S_dma_virt;</p>
<p>*ppbyVirtAddrUncached = (EC_T_BYTE*)S_dma_virt;</p>
<p>*pqwPhysAddr = S_dma_phys;</p>
<p>}</p>
<p>```</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With this change I could successfully run the EcMasterDemo</p>
