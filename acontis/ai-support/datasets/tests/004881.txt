### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
EMI DC/ClockAdjustment/Visible ignored

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>We need the Clock Adjustment section of the Distributed Clocks master tab to be hidden. I expected that the Visible attribute on the Clock Adjustment property in the EMI to do this, but it is ignored.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;PropertyGroup Name="DC" Visible="1"&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Property Name="ClockAdjustment" Visible="0" Fixed="1"&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Value DataType="Uint32"&gt;1&lt;/Value&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;/Property&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Would you consider supporting this in the standard EcView/EcViewModel? If not, then we will just hide it in our codebase. The change was quite trivial:</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In MasterCfgDCTabView.xaml:</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;!-- Mode --&gt;</p>
<p><span style="color:red;">- &lt;Grid Grid.Column="1" Grid.Row="3"&gt;</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ &lt;Grid Grid.Column="1" Grid.Row="3"</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ Visibility="{Binding IsClockAdjustmentVisible, Converter={StaticResource booleanToVisibilityConverter}, ConverterParameter='collapse'}"&gt;</span></p>
<p> </p>
<p>In CfgMasterDCViewModel.cs:</p>
<p> </p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ /// &lt;summary&gt;</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ /// IsClockAdjustmentVisible</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ /// &lt;/summary&gt;</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ public bool IsClockAdjustmentVisible</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ {</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ get { return Device.Emi.DcGrp["ClockAdjustment"].Visible; }</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+ }</span></p>
<p> </p>
<p>Thank you.</p>
