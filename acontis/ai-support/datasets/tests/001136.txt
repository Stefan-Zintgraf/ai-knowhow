### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
Large number of ecatCoeSdoDownloadReq?

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi Asa,<div><br></div><div>unfortunately, you cannot queue 1000 Sdo Transfers. There is a hard-coded limit of 20 per slave for memory usage protection. You can loop as long as the <span>ecatCoeSdoDownloadReq</span><span> returns EC_E_NOERROR, then sleep e.g. 10 cycles and retry. Please note that only 1 pending transfer per slave and mbx type is possible.</span>
