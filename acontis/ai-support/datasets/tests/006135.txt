### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
The question about EtherCAT diagnostic with procentec device

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi John,<div><br></div><div>I know the Procentec software a little. They require the master independent diagnosis interface ETG.1510.</div><div>
<div><a href="https://www.acontis.com/en/monitoring-and-diagnosis-software.html" title="https://www.acontis.com/en/monitoring-and-diagnosis-software.html" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://www.acontis.com/en/monitoring-and-diagnosis-software.html</a></div>
<div><div><a href="https://www.ethercat.org/en/downloads/downloads_BC1B4EC2069B44A8969ACEE56715D557.htm" title="https://www.ethercat.org/en/downloads/downloads_BC1B4EC2069B44A8969ACEE56715D557.htm" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://www.ethercat.org/en/downloads/downloads_BC1B4EC2069B44A8969ACEE56715D557.htm</a></div></div>
<div><br></div>
<div>That´s based on the UDP Mailbox gateway. We support it and also TwinCAT. I am not aware about our masters.</div>
<div>EC-Master: This a feature pack and not purchased by many customers, below 5.</div>
<div>
<div>Slides 50ff.</div>
<div><a href="https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1021_V1.1_EC-Master-Feature-Packs.pdf" title="https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1021_V1.1_EC-Master-Feature-Packs.pdf" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1021_V1.1_EC-Master-Feature-Packs.pdf</a></div>
</div>
<div><br></div>
<div><br></div>
<div>TwinCAT: By default disabled and not supported by older versions. Don´t know the details</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Therefore the number of potential customers which can use the Procentec tools are limited.</div>
<div>Also: ETG.1510 is a pure diagnosis interface for errors and states. No process data! </div>
<div><br></div>
<div>In the past we talked to several people at Procentec, if they might be interested in licensing EC-Monitor. <span>But they had no interest.</span>
</div>
<div><span>Today I am not sure, we should sell EC-Monitor to them, because EC-Inspector is very successful.</span></div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Please let me know your further questions.</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Best Regards</div>
