### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi Leslie,<div><br></div><div>yes the frames are send better on time and the timestamps send within the frames are more accurate. Time triggered send (TTS) allows the frame to be send in a timely manner handled by the NIC hardware. Also the usually more accurate NIC clock instead of the CPU clock is used.</div><div><br></div><div>Drawbacks: A bit more implementation/configuration effort. Just a few NICs support TTS. Currently we just support two of them</div><div>
<div><a href="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-b/emllicss.html#tts-feature" title="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-b/emllicss.html#tts-feature" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-b/emllicss.html#tts-feature</a></div>
