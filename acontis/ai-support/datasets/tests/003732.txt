<div>
<p>Hi,</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Can you please help the below questions?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>1) I wonder how the master generates slave id. Is the below assumption is correct</p>
<p> </p>
<p>If slaves in an ENI as following</p>
<p>· PhysAddr = 1001, AutoIncAddr = 0</p>
<p>· PhysAddr = 1002, AutoIncAddr = 65535 (-1)</p>
<p>· PhysAddr = 1003, AutoIncAddr = 65534 (-2)</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Then</p>
<p>· ecatGetCfgSlaveInfo(1, 1001, &amp;config_slave);</p>
<p><b>=&gt;slaveId = 0</b></p>
<p>· ecatGetCfgSlaveInfo(1, 1002, &amp;config_slave);</p>
<p><b>=&gt;slaveId = 1</b></p>
<p>· ecatGetCfgSlaveInfo(1, 1003, &amp;config_slave);</p>
<p><b>=&gt;slaveId = 2</b></p>
<p> </p>
<p>2)Shall a slave id always be less than ecatGetNumberConfigSlaves() ?</p>
<p>From the above example where an ENI has 3 slaves, then slave ids are in the range of 0 to 2</p>
<p> </p>
<p>3)Are EC_T_CFG_SLAVE_INFO<span style="color:#010001;">::dwSlaveId And EC_T_BUS_SLAVE_INFO::dwSlaveId the same? If not, what is the difference? And when/how can EC_T_BUS_SLAVE_INFO::dwSlaveId be used?</span></p>
<p> </p>
