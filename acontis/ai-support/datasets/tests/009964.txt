Hi Yuichi,<div><br></div><div>1.) 

DCM require a stable and accurate cycle / time base. This is not always available under Windows. That's why BusShift was not working. An optimized link layer may help. But these modes are mostly for testing under Windows. If it works --&gt; fine, but often it will not work</div><div><br></div><div>2.) In EC-Engineer at the moment not. EC-Master has an API (

emDcmGetStatus ). In EC-Engineer you will get an error if not everything works. But no status</div><div><br></div><div>For your other questions: 

<a href="https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1020_V1.3_EC-Master-Technical.pdf" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1020_V1.3_EC-Master-Technical.pdf</a> Page 27 - 35</div><div><br></div><div>Here is everything described.</div><div><br></div><div>Best regards,</div><div>Kai<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
