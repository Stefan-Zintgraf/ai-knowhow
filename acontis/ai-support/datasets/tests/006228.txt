EtherCAT Cycle Drop in QNX

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>Just dropping you another sticky note we have about something that's been bugging us. So, one of our customers is writing to us with an issue in the EtherCAT master scene. Apparently, every now and then, it's like the master decides to skip a beat and miss out on 1 cycle. This little hiccup is causing some control errors on their loop. Now, they are claiming this only happens in QNX, while everything's good with On Time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>So, I have 2 question now:</p>
<ol start="1" type="1">
<li>Does the EcMaster have a notification to show this event?, like a heads-up the moment the master decides to ghost a cycle. </li>
</ol>
<p> </p>
<ol start="2" type="1">
<li>Are you aware of this case in QNX? If so, what tool do you recommend troubleshooting this?</li>
</ol>
<p> </p>
<p>Thank you again!</p>
