<p> </p>
<p>The manual says “this function may not be called from within the JobTask’s context”. Does this mean do not the function while JobTask is executing or do not call from within the JobTask thread.</p>
<p>[CER] The function emCoeSdoUpload is blocking until the transfer is done, but the transfer is processed within the jobs. If this function is called in the context of the job task, it will produce a dead lock situation.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>2. This comes from our colleagues in Japan.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Does the EC Master stack have a retransmission frame mechanism if the frame with CRC error returns?</p>
<p>[CER] Yes</p>
<p>The return frame of some emCoeSdoUpload and PDO frames have a CRC error</p>
<p>– No.745 frame has a CRC error of emCoeSdoUpload</p>
<p>• A retransmission frame seems transmitted after 20ms but the source address is different, so I wonder who sent this frame (=No.902)</p>
<p>– No.745 source address: 02 00 0a bc 08 d8</p>
<p>– No.902 source address: 02 01 0a bc 08 d8</p>
