Question about EC-Master(Murata Machinery) [071]

We would like to confirm a few points regarding the cycle time explanation for your EC-Master product.
In the user manual, under section "3.1.1 Standard Timing: Short output dead time", the diagram titled "Cyclic and acyclic frames" seems to show that the frame sent in Cycle1 by Job S is received in Cycle2 by Job P.
Based on this, is it correct to understand that the available time between sending a frame and receiving it in the next cycle is not the full cycle time, but rather the cycle time minus the App.Task duration?
We have outlined our concerns in detail, including the diagram, on Sheet No. 71 for your reference.

Best regards,
Takuya Fujikawa
