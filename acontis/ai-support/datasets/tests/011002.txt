### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
EC_T_USER_JOB::eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames taking a long time to return

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>Hi,</div><div> </div><div>We are using the Class A Linux stack for EC Master and sometimes see Frame Loss notifications while running a 250uS cycle time. When profiling our PDO loop logic we have noticed that in the frames that overrun our 250uS cycle time, that it is the eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames job that is taking 250-300uS to execute just by itself. 99% of the time it takes the expected amount of time, but sometimes it takes extremely long and we see frame loss/overruns as a result.</div><div> </div><div>I was curious as to what could cause this job to take this long? We don't have any custom user logic in this timestamp, it is just before and after the SendAllCycFrames execjob() call.</div><div> </div><div>We also sometimes see after some frame loss events that we get a NOT_ALL_OPERATIONAL notification. Meaning one or more of our nodes are dropping out of operational into safe op. I am wondering if you have any thoughts on if the frame loss could lead to this either with communication issues or DC clock synchronization issues.</div><div> </div><div>But at the end of the day we are trying to investigate why there is frame loss in the first place and why the eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames could take so long sometimes. We have pretty significant runtime with this software and typically dont see this. Could this be from a hardware issues? What hardware interation does eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames have that it could potentially be blocking for a long time?</div><div> </div><div>Any help is appreciated.</div><div> </div><div>Thanks</div><div> </div><div>
