Dear EtherCAT Support

Question about EC-Master(YUSHIN) [E151]

  1. Transition the EtherCAT slave from OP state to INIT state (executed by a task other than the job task)
  2. Execute emDeinitMaster() (executed in the job task)
  3. Reboot the μC3

The customer is starting the master following the procedure described above, but an exception error is occurring.
By tracing the process through debugging, we found that the execution flow moves to the CEcTimer::ResetInstance function, and then an exception error occurs during a malloc-related process.
Therefore, we would like to ask for your guidance on the following two points:

Q1. Is it normal for the CEcTimer::ResetInstance function to be called from within the emDeinitMaster function?

Q2. In the process of either the emDeinitMaster function or the CEcTimer::ResetInstance function, is the malloc or free function called?


Best regards,
Takuya Fujikawa
