Hi Daniel,<div><br></div><div>WKC errors would be generated under the following circumstances:</div><div><br></div><div>1- If some slaves are missing on the network</div><div><br></div><div>2- Slaves are in error state</div><div><br></div><div>3- Sync managers are configured <span>in mailbox mode and the slave firmware access to the process data during the cyclic frame processing.</span>
</div><div>a- They only appears during startup in transition (mostly SO)</div><div>-&gt; Could be ignored</div><div>
<span>b- They appears in OP cyclically (typically every 4-5s)</span><br>
</div><div><span>-&gt; Using DC and ShiftTime can adjust the position of the SYNC signal and move it in a "safe area"</span></div><div>c- They appears sometimes</div><div>-&gt; Real-time violation or some jitter applied to the cyclic frames within the cycle</div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards,</div><div>
