Hi Harry,<div><br></div><div>during the transition, we ignore the WKC errors. They are notified, after the transition was successful. In this case some slaves could still generate a WKC error, because their sync manager is configured in mailbox and not triple buffer mode. You can check it in EC-Engineer:</div><div>
<img style="max-width:100%;width: 855.011px;max-width: 100%;max-height: 551.011px;" src="/api/v1/ticket_attachment/2871/15195/59746?view=inline"><br>
</div><div>In the column Buffer mode, 1 means mailbox mode.</div><div><br></div><div>If this mode is used for the in/outputs, WKC error can happen if the slave firmware is accessing the process data during the processing time of the cyclic frame in the ESC.</div><div>Some slave manufacturer, "calibrate" their firmware and adjust this access at startup. It can be related to SYNC signal and DCM too.</div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards,</div><div>
