### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
EC-Master timeout when trying to go from PreOp to SafeOp

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div dir="ltr">Hi,<div> </div>
<div>I'm running into an issue with a new setup. The setup consists of a 3 port junction with 3 CiA402 drives at the two output ports, thus a total of 7 devices. I've configured DC, which seems to work, but every approx. 3min and 30 to 50 seconds the drives make a rattling noise for a few seconds. We believe this might be a timing issue, i.e. the reference clock (1st drive) and our master clock drifting away. So I wanted to enable DCM (BUS_SHIFT) to synchronize the reference clock to our master. However, during initialization of the master, our implementation reached Init, it then requires ~900 cycles to reach PreOp and then I get a timeout when trying to reach SafeOp. I've already set the transition timeout to 15 seconds, but I still get the timeout.<br><br>Is there anything I can do to fix this? I've already tried setting the bushift's `dwInSyncLimit` to half the cycle time, as well as setting `bUseDcLoopCtlStdValues` to `EC_TRUE` without success. The cycle time of the system is 8ms.</div>
<div> </div>
<div>Any pointers would be appreciated.</div>
