<div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
<div>Dear Sawada-san,</div>
<div><br></div>
<div><span>CYCCMD_WKC_ERROR</span></div>
<div>-&gt; No. only acyclic frames are repeated.</div>
<div><br></div>
<div><span>MASTER_INITCMD_WKC_ERROR</span></div>
<div><span>SLAVE_INITCMD_WKC_ERROR</span></div>
<div>
<span>-&gt; The retry is pending and will be executed by EC-Master automatically, according </span><span>dwEcatCmdMaxRetries</span>
</div>
<div><span><br></span></div>
<div><span>SLAVE_INITCMD_RESPONSE_ERROR</span></div>
<div><span>MBSLAVE_INITCMD_TIMEOUT</span></div>
<div><span>MASTER_INITCMD_RESPONSE_ERROR</span></div>
<div><span>-&gt; The retries have been executed and EC-Master give up with error.</span></div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Permanent error can point to defect slave or master system. They are probably not automatically recoverable and need some action from the operator. emRescueScan() can help to recover for a permanent frame loss. emBadConnectionDetect() can help to detect defect slaves or cable.</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Your application should consider to support (or not) some missed cycles and not immediately give up.       </div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Best Regards,</div>
<div>Cyril</div>
