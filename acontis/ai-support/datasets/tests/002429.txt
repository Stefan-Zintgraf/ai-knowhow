<div>Hi Jon,</div><div><br></div><div>for a data type of one byte (EC_T_BYTE/EC_T_SBYTE) he can directly access the value without a macro.</div><div><br></div><div><div>The macros are only required for data types greater than 1 byte, since the bytes may have to be swapped. Since EtherCAT is little endian, the swap is only required on big endian systems (e.g. PowerPC).</div></div><div><div>The macro EC-GETBITS only makes sense for special data types with few bits.</div></div><div>See also our manual about this:</div><div><div><a href="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.1/html/ec-master-class-b/software-integration.html#process-data-access-functions" title="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.1/html/ec-master-class-b/software-integration.html#process-data-access-functions" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.1/html/ec-master-class-b/software-integration.html#process-data-access-functions</a></div></div><div><div>If something is missing or unclear in the manual, I'm happy to change it.</div></div><div><br></div><div></div><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
