### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
Proper EtherCAT State Machine Auto-recovery to OP

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>Hello,</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I have a system that I would like to recovery automatically from various errors, including power outages.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>My maindevice is backed by a UPS but my subdevices are not, and I would like to detect bad states and automatically recover to OP.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>My current implementation is basically the following pseudocode:</p>
<p> </p>
<p>while true:</p>
<p>setMasterState(OP)</p>
<p>sleep(5)</p>
<p> </p>
<p>This works, but I think it is wrong since I have seen modules lose their CoE init settings on power cycle.</p>
<p> </p>
<ol start="1" type="1">
<li>Does setMasterState perform jumping transitions (transitions that skip CoE init commands)?</li>
<li>Do I need to change to more of a state machine approach where I call transitions for specific subdevices according to what state they are in?</li>
<ol start="1" type="a">
<li>For example, cyclically monitor the state of all subdevices using GetSlaveState and command a single transition at a time upwards towards op:</li>
</ol>
</ol>
<p> </p>
<p>While true:</p>
<p>For subdevice in subdevices:</p>
<p>getSlaveState(subdevice)</p>
<p>transition_towards_op(subdevice)</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Thanks,</p>
<p>Jeff Anderson</p>
<p> </p>
</div>
