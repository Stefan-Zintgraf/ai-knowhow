Hi <span>Misha,</span><div><br></div><div>the DC burst is related to DC and not DCM. It is need to compensate the static drift between the slaves. You can change it by calling emDcConfigure() and change <span>EC_T_DC_CONFIGURE::</span><span>dwTotalBurstLength</span>
</div><div><br></div><div><div><a href="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-a/dc_api.html?highlight=burst#emdcconfigure" title="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-a/dc_api.html?highlight=burst#emdcconfigure" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-a/dc_api.html?highlight=burst#emdcconfigure</a></div></div><div><br></div><div>Using a shorter burst will speed up the startup procedure but it will takes several seconds (10s-20s) to see that the slaves SYNC0 are InSync.</div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards,<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
