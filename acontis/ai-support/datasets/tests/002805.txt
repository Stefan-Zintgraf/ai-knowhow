### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
Bug in NotifyDcmSync() logging

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>I noticed a simple bug with logging for the CEcMaster::NotifyDcmSync() method. Two non-existent values were logged (nTimeStampDiff and dwErrorCode), which confused me. Here is my proposed fix:</p>
<p> </p>
<p>EC_T_VOID CEcMaster::NotifyDcmSync(EC_T_BOOL bInSync, EC_T_INT nCtlErrorNsecCur, EC_T_INT nCtlErrorNsecAvg, EC_T_INT nCtlErrorNsecMax)</p>
<p>{</p>
<p>EC_T_NOTIFICATION_INTDESC* pNotification = EC_NULL;</p>
<p>EC_T_DCM_SYNC_NTFY_DESC* pNotifyDesc = EC_NULL;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>if (!IsNotificationEnabled(EC_NOTIFY_DCM_SYNC))</p>
<p>return;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>pNotification = (EC_T_NOTIFICATION_INTDESC*)AllocNotification();</p>
<p>if (EC_NULL == pNotification)</p>
<p>return;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>pNotifyDesc = &amp;(pNotification-&gt;uNotification.Notification.desc.DcmInSyncDesc);</p>
<p> </p>
<p>pNotifyDesc-&gt;IsInSync = bInSync;</p>
<p>pNotifyDesc-&gt;nCtlErrorNsecCur = nCtlErrorNsecCur;</p>
<p>pNotifyDesc-&gt;nCtlErrorNsecAvg = nCtlErrorNsecAvg;</p>
<p>pNotifyDesc-&gt;nCtlErrorNsecMax = nCtlErrorNsecMax;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>+ EcLogMsg(EC_LOG_LEVEL_INFO, (pEcLogContext, EC_LOG_LEVEL_INFO, "Notify EC_NOTIFY_DCM_SYNC(IsInSync %d, nCtlErrorNsecCur %d, nCtlErrorNsecAvg %d, nCtlErrorNsecMax %d)\n",</p>
<p>- EcLogMsg(EC_LOG_LEVEL_INFO, (pEcLogContext, EC_LOG_LEVEL_INFO, "Notify EC_NOTIFY_DCM_SYNC(IsInSync %d, nCtlErrorNsecCur %d, nCtlErrorNsecAvg %d, nCtlErrorNsecMax %d, nTimeStampDiff %d, dwErrorCode 0x%x)\n",</p>
<p>pNotifyDesc-&gt;IsInSync, pNotifyDesc-&gt;nCtlErrorNsecCur, pNotifyDesc-&gt;nCtlErrorNsecAvg, pNotifyDesc-&gt;nCtlErrorNsecMax));</p>
<p> </p>
<p>NotifyAndFree(EC_NOTIFY_DCM_SYNC, pNotification, sizeof(EC_T_DCM_SYNC_NTFY_DESC));</p>
<p>}</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Quinton Tormanen</p>
