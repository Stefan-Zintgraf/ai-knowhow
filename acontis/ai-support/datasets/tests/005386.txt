### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
Question about "State transition processing" from YAMAHA.

### ARTICLE00_BODY
Dear EtherCAT Support

We received a notification from Yamaha that the following processes are taking longer than expected:

   ecatInitMaster -> 2.5 sec
   ecatSetMasterState INIT to PREOP -> 1 sec
   ecatSetMasterState PREOP to SAFEOP -> 3 sec

Regarding "ecatSetMasterState," it seems that the delay is caused by waiting for the SLAVE transition, and we have asked for confirmation whether TwinCAT experiences the same delay.

However, we believe that "ecatInitMaster" is not caused by the SLAVE.

Q1. Is "ecatInitMaster" caused by the SLAVE?
Q2. (If Q1 is yes) What process is being performed during "ecatInitMaster"?

Thank you for your confirmation.

-------------------------------------------------------
----- The Japanese translation is as follows: -----
-------------------------------------------------------

ヤマハよりそれぞれ以下処理で時間が掛かっているとの連絡が御座いました。

   ecatInitMaster -> 2.5 sec
   ecatSetMasterState INIT to PREOP  -> 1 sec
   ecatSetMasterState PREOP to SAFEOP  -> 3 sec

"ecatSetMasterState"については、SLAVE側の遷移待ちが要因と思われ、TwinCATでも同じ時間が掛かるか確認をしてもらっています。

しかし、"ecatInitMaster"はSLAVE要因では無いと思います。

Q1.ecatInitMasterはSLAVE要因でしょうか？
Q2.(If Q1 yes)どのような処理がされているのでしょうか？
