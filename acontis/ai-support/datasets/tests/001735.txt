<div>Hi Oleg,</div><div><br></div><div>I'm going well thanks!</div><div><br></div><div>We support the following device IDs, in fact Synopsys IP Cores:</div><div>
<span>#define PCI_DEVICE_TSNGBE                     ((EC_T_WORD)0x4BB0)     /**&lt; (0x8086, 0x4BB0) */</span><br>
</div><div>#define PCI_DEVICE_TSNGBE_2                 ((EC_T_WORD)0x4BA0)     /**&lt; (0x8086, 0x4BA0) */</div><div>#define PCI_DEVICE_TSNGBE_3                 ((EC_T_WORD)0x4B32)     /**&lt; (0x8086, 0x4B32) */</div><div><br></div><div>At least we can run EtherCAT with our link-layer, but it is of course a specific use case, very different from standard networking.</div><div>I think that the RTOS-32 drivers are based on the BSD drivers. Perhaps Intel doesn't provide it, I don't know.
