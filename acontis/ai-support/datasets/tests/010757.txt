### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi,<div><br></div><div>normally this is caused when you do an SDO upload from an object which does not exists.</div><div>Is your slave in PREOP? You can access the CoE OD only if the slave is in PREOP or above.</div><div><br></div><div>Can you enable our GUI debug level?</div><div><div>EcDefaultSettings.<span>GuiDebugMessageLevel = ELogLevel.EFull</span>
</div></div><div><span><br></span></div><div><span>... this writes all calls to our .NET Wrapper into the logfile.</span></div><div><span><br></span></div><div><span>... afterwards you should see in the log file which index/subindex was accessed.</span></div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards,</div><div>Martin<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
