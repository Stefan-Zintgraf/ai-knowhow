### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>I am confused by the following highlighted references in TabPageResources.xaml:</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;ResourceDictionary xmlns=<a href="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml/presentation" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml/presentation</a></p>
<p> xmlns:x=<a href="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml</a></p>
<p>xmlns:vm="clr-namespace:EcViewModel;assembly=EcViewModel"</p>
<p> xmlns:igEditors=<a href="http://infragistics.com/Editors" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://infragistics.com/Editors</a>&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;Style TargetType="{x:Type igEditors:XamCheckEditor}" &gt;</p>
<p>&lt;!-- AS 1/13/12 TFS69479 Added Padding --&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Setter Property="Padding" Value="0" /&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;/Style&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;vm:ImageConverter x:Key="imgConverter"/&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;vm:EnumMatchToBooleanConverter x:Key="enumConverter"/&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;vm:IntegerAsHexAndDecimalConverter x:Key="intAsHexAndDecimalConverter"/&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;/ResourceDictionary&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>That is, I don’t see any implementation of ImageConverter or IntegerAsHexAndDecimalConverter in EcViewModel. I ran into this when playing around with upgrading the EcView project to the new SDK project format. Perhaps it is more strict? Or perhaps this file was not being built?</p>
