### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
Recommended handling of Bus Mismatch before going to OP

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi <span>Kendall,</span><div><br></div><div>the handling bus mismatch is depending on the application and the meaning of the missing slaves or tolerance of unexpected slaves. It depends on the error notification and on the operator availability.</div><div><br></div><div>We have a lot of customers running emScanBus() in a loop during the startup, because some slaves need more or less time to power on.</div><div><br></div><div>It is possible to run in a "degraded" state by calling EC_IOCTL_ALL_SLAVES_MUST_REACH_MASTER_STATE:</div><div><div><a href="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-b/api.html#emiocontrol-ec-ioctl-all-slaves-must-reach-master-state" title="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-b/api.html#emiocontrol-ec-ioctl-all-slaves-must-reach-master-state" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.2/html/ec-master-class-b/api.html#emiocontrol-ec-ioctl-all-slaves-must-reach-master-state</a></div></div><div><br></div><div>Using SuperSetENI (Page 17) / Hot Connect <span>(Page 10) </span><span>can be way to deal within missing slaves:</span>
</div><div><div><a href="https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1021_V1.1_EC-Master-Feature-Packs.pdf" title="https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1021_V1.1_EC-Master-Feature-Packs.pdf" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://software.acontis.com/Documents/AT1021_V1.1_EC-Master-Feature-Packs.pdf</a></div></div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards,<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
