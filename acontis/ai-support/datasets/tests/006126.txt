### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
[Chieftek]assign slave as reference clock

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi,<div><br></div><div>yes, normally we use the first DC slave as reference clock.</div><div><br></div><div>If you want to use another slave as reference clock you can do:</div><div>myEcDevice.<span>SettingsData.Settings.ManualRefClock </span><span>= 1003 // Your slave address</span>
</div><div>
<span>myEcDevice</span>.<span>SettingsData.</span><span>Write(</span>myEcDevice.SettingsData.Settings<span>);</span>
