Question about EC-Master(SHINKAWA)

What the customer wants to achieve:
  1. If the number of slaves actually connected is fewer than the number indicated in the ENI file, the system will operate with the slaves that are recognized.
  2. If the number of slaves actually connected is greater than the number indicated in the ENI file,

Q1. Is No1 possible to achieve with FP hot connect?
Q2. Is there a way to achieve No2?
