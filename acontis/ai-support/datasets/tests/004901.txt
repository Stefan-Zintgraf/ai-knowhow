Dear EtherCAT Support

Question about EC-Master(Mitsutoyo)

The customer wants to change the jobtask flow as follows.

Change before:
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_ProcessAllRxFrames, &oJobParms);
  myAppWorkpd(pAppContext);
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames, &oJobParms);
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_MasterTimer, EC_NULL);
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_SendAcycFrames, EC_NULL);

Change after:
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_ProcessAllRxFrames, &oJobParms);
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames, &oJobParms);
  myAppWorkpd(pAppContext);
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_MasterTimer, EC_NULL);
  ecatExecJob(eUsrJob_SendAcycFrames, EC_NULL);

- The reason they want to change it is because myAppWorkpd has a lot of processing time jitter.
- It is acceptable that this change delays the transmission by one cycle.

Q. Is there a problem with their master usage?

Best regards,
Takuya Fujikawa
