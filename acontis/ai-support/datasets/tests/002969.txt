Memory Usage problem with V3.1.3.3

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>I was looking at the new Memory Usage data showed on the Master General tab in Diagnosis mode. I am connecting over RAS to our EC-Master. I was getting bizarre values for memory usage like the following:</p>
<p> </p>
<p><img style="max-width:100%;width:7.4895in;max-height:4.0in;width:719px;max-height:384px;" src="/api/v1/ticket_attachment/2969/15625/61628?view=inline"></p>
<p> </p>
<p>When I looked at this more, I see this call stack:</p>
<p> </p>
<p> <b>EcCore.dll:</b> EcCore.EcDevice.GetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>EcCore.dll:</b> EcCore.EcMasterUnit.GetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>EcCore.dll:</b> EcCore.EcMasterWithLogging.GetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>EcCore.dll:</b> EcCore.EcMasterDotNetImpl.GetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>EcWrapperDotNet.dll:</b> EcWrapperDotNet.CEcWrapperDotNet.GetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>AtEmRasClnt.dll:</b> ecwGetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>AtEmRasClnt.dll:</b> emGetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>AtEmRasClnt.dll:</b> AtemRasClnt::CAtEmRasClientWrapper::GetMemoryUsage()</p>
<p> <b>AtEmRasClnt.dll:</b> CEcMasterInterface::CAtEmRasClntInterface::GetMemLog()</p>
<p> </p>
<p>What I find for the implementation of <b>CAtEmRasClntInterface::GetMemLog()</b>:</p>
<p> </p>
<p><span style="color:blue;"> class</span>
<span style="color:#2b91af;">CAtEmRasClntInterface</span> : <span style="color:blue;">public</span>
<span style="color:#2b91af;">CEcMasterInterfaceBase</span></p>
<p>{</p>
<p><span style="color:blue;"> public</span>:</p>
<p>...</p>
<p> </p>
<p> <span style="color:blue;">struct</span>
<span style="color:#2b91af;">_EC_T_MEMORY_LOGGER</span>* GetMemLog() { <span style="color:blue;">return</span> &amp;m_oMemLog; }</p>
<p> <span style="color:blue;">struct</span>
<span style="color:#2b91af;">_EC_T_MEMORY_LOGGER</span> m_oMemLog;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>...</p>
<p>};</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I do not see m_oMemLog get initialized, nor do I see it written to anywhere. Therefore, in my debug builds, all of its members are initialized to 0xCDCDCDCD, which has a decimal of 3,452,816,845, which when divided by 1000, gives the “3452816” value shown in my screen shot above.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>What is supposed to happen in this situation where ERemote is being used?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Thank you.</p>
