<p>Dear Support Team</p>

<p>Our customer has informed us that the following error occurs during operation.</p>
<p>EC_NOTIFY_SLAVE_ERROR_STATUS_INFO</p>
<p>The log has not been acquired.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It seems that wStatus was 0x20 and wStatusCode was 0x18 when the error occurred.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>Under what conditions does this error occur?</p>
<p> </p>

<p>It was "No valid inputs available".</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Is it in a state where input cannot be obtained?</p>
<p>Is it possible that the wiring required to obtain the input value is unstable?</p>
