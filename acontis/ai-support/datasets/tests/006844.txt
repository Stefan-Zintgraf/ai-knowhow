### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
OsSocketGetLastError() for INTEGRITY

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>Is there a reason why OsSocketGetLastError() is defined as ‘<b>0</b>’ in INTEGRITY\EcOsPlatform.h instead of ‘<b>errno</b>’?</p>
<p> </p>
<p><span style="color:gray;">#ifdef</span>
<span style="color:#6f008a;">EC_SOCKET_IP_SUPPORTED</span></p>
<p><span style="color:gray;">#define</span>
<span style="color:#6f008a;">INVALID_SOCKET</span> (-1)</p>
<p><span style="color:gray;">#define</span>
<span style="color:#6f008a;">SOCKET_ERROR</span> (-1)</p>
<p><span style="color:red;">-#define OsSocketGetLastError() 0</span></p>
<p><span style="color:red;">+#define OsSocketGetLastError() errno</span></p>
<p><span style="color:gray;">#endif</span>
<span style="color:green;">/* EC_SOCKET_IP_SUPPORTED */</span></p>
<p> </p>
<p>It appears to compile just fine for me at least.</p>
