<p><span lang="EN-US"><a href="https://docs.ros.org/en/dashing/Tutorials/Real-Time-Programming.html" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://docs.ros.org/en/dashing/Tutorials/Real-Time-Programming.html</a></span></p>

<p><span lang="EN-US">If ROS can dispatch messages faster than 1ms and guarantee the real-time behavior. It could work.</span></p>
