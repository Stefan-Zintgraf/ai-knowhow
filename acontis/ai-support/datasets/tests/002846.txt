### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
Max Acyclic Frames that can be queued

### ARTICLE01_BODY
<div>
<p>Hi Jon,</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Some of our DDI EtherCAT modules use COE communication for asynchronous updates. By using COE for these EtherCAT modules we avoid unnecessary process data allocations. We noticed during stress-testing (greater than 20 threads) this slot-card that we were hitting the MAX_QUEUED_COE_CMDS limit.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>DDI provides a wrapper to abstract the COE functionality from the end user. We can possibly retry or queue the COE messages ourselves, we don’t believe an update to the Acontis library is required at this time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Is the MAX_QUEUED_COE_CMDS a global limit or is this per-EtherCAT master instance?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Thank you,</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shaun</p>
