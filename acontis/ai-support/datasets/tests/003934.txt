<div>Dear Sawada-san,</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Q1.</div>
<div>to detect the DL status interrupt, EC-Master has to be at least in PREOP. At the beginning, just after emInitMaster(), the application needs to call emScanBus to detect changes on the network if EC-Master is not configured or not in PREOP. </div>
<div>The recovery procedure is more simple if it contains emScanBus in case of slave deconnection / reconnection. Without ths call, you need to refresh some variable and react on notification.</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>Q2.</div>
<div>Yes you can pass <span>EC_WAITINFINITE.</span>
</div>
