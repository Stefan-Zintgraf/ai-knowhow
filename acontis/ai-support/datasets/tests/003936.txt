Dear Sawada-san,<div><span><br></span></div><div>
<span>if  a slave becomes defect and destroy all the received frames, you have a permanent frame loss circumstance. In this case, you will receive </span><span>EC_NOTIFY_FRAME_RESPONSE_ERROR, and </span><span>emGetNumConnectedSlaves() will return 0, even if the link is connected. Calling emRescueScan at this time can fix the issue and isolate the defect slave.</span><br>
