### ARTICLE00_BODY
Hi,<div> </div><div>A customer has a device that has something wired incorrectly because when they are connected to the IN port, it is not responsive, and when on the OUT port, EC-Engineer complains about the line crossed issue. They say that in TwinCAT they can connect to the OUT port, receive no line crossed error, and communicate with the device. So they are looking for a way to connect on the OUT port with EC-Engineer/EC-Master and still read values from the drive.</div><div> </div><div>1. Is there a way they can suppress/ignore the line crossed error and continue?</div><div> </div><div>2. Do you know why TwinCAT wouldn’t report this line-crossed error like EC-Engineer does?<br><div>

### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
Line Crossed Errors

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi Jon,<div><br></div><div>which customer is it? Do they have EC-Master?<br><div><br></div>
<div>1. Yes with EC-Master it is possible via an IO control:</div>
<div><span>EC_T_BOOL lineCrossedError = EC_FALSE;</span></div>
<div>
<div>dwRes = ecatIoCtl(EC_IOCTL_SB_SET_ERROR_ON_CROSSED_LINES, &amp;lineCrossedError, sizeof(EC_T_BOOL), EC_NULL, 0, EC_NULL);</div>
<div><a href="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.1/html/ec-master-class-b/api_busscan.html#emiocontrol-ec-ioctl-sb-set-error-on-crossed-lines" title="https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.1/html/ec-master-class-b/api_busscan.html#emiocontrol-ec-ioctl-sb-set-error-on-crossed-lines" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">https://public.acontis.com/manuals/EC-Master/3.1/html/ec-master-class-b/api_busscan.html#emiocontrol-ec-ioctl-sb-set-error-on-crossed-lines</a></div>
<div>They can deactivate reporting this error in the their EC-Master instance and connect EC-Engineer via RAS.</div>
<div><br></div>
<div>2. Maybe Beckhoff has a different default. Our default is to report this issue. EC-Engineer has a Line Crossed Analyzer. To us it is important to detected cabling issues. As you might have a different topology than you expect.</div>
