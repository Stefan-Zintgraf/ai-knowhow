Dear Sawada-san,<div><br></div><div>Q1. I<span>t depends on the error. I can imagine that going to SAFEOP is enough. You can read SDO and PDO for diagnostic. In PREOP, you cannot access to the PDO, only SDO. In INIT you don't have any diagnostic on the slave application level, only EtherCAT topology and status.</span>
</div><div><span>Q2. I don't think so. PREOP is a good state for diagnostic. It can make sense to go to INIT and to OP again. In case of recovery. SO the slaves have a chance to "restart" from INIT.</span></div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards,<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
