### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
Method to get the Sync0 time

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>Hi,</p>
<p>A few months ago, I sent you the formula we are using to get the Sync 0time (as we need this time to have accurate node Output and input times).</p>
<p>I wanted you to confirm that this is the correct formula, and you confirmed it (1<sup>st</sup> question in the attached e-mail).</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The formula that I sent you (and that you confirmed that it is correct) is:</p>
<p>///////////////////////////////////</p>
<p><i>nodeSync0Time = busTime - pnDiffCur - cycleTime + Sync0Shift;</i></p>
<p><i>where:</i></p>
<p><i>busTime is the value from ecatGetBusTime(busTime)</i></p>
<p><i>pnDiffCur is the value from ecatDcmGetStatus(pdwErrorCode, pnDiffCur, pnDiffAvg, pnDiffMax)</i></p>
<p><i>cycleTime = 1ms</i></p>
<p><i>Sync0Shift = 300 MicroSeconds (30% of cycletime)</i></p>
<p>///////////////////////////////////</p>
<p> </p>
<p>According to my tests (and calculations), we should add nDiffCur instead of subtracting it.</p>
<p>Shouldn’t the formula be:<br>
<b>nodeSync0Time = busTime </b><b>+ pnDiffCur - cycleTime + Sync0Shift;</b></p>
<p> </p>
<p>If we should really subtract, then I have another question and it relates to tests that we have where from time to time(random time between 10 minutes to an hour), the computed time value of <i>nodeSync0Time </i>is earlier than the last computed value (which points to an incorrect behavior as this value should be incrementing)</p>
<p>Iteration (n): nodeSync0Time: 674841800085524, busTimeUs: 674841800086229, nDiffCur: 5</p>
<p>Iteration (n-1): nodeSync0Time: 674841800085533, busTimeUs: 674841800085734, nDiffCur: -499</p>
<p>If we add the nDiffCur in our calculation (instead of subtracting it), then the nodeSync0Time of (n-1) would be OK</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Can you please confirm if we should subtract or add nDiffCur in our formula?</p>
<p>Also, if we really should subtract it, would you know what would cause a Sync0 time that is very near the Sync0 time of the last cycle? Is this behavior somewhat expected?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Thanks,</p>
<p>Naji</p>
