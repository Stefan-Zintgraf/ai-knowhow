### ARTICLE01_SUBJECT
[Chieftek]advanced option--Potential Reference Clock

### ARTICLE01_BODY
Hi,<div><br></div><div>Potential Ref Clock is a setting which comes from the ESI file. It tells if a slave is allowed to be a potential ref clock. </div><div>So PotentialRefClock controls if the checkbox is enabled and ActivateAsPotentialRefClock controls if the check box is checked by the user. </div><div>If there is no PotentialRefClock setting in ESI we enable the checkbox.</div><div><br></div><div>Best regards,</div><div>Kai<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
