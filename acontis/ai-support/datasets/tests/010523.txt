Hi,<div><br></div><div>HotConnect is a feature pack which has to be enabled / activated / bought for the EtherCAT Master. You have to contact dSpace for that. </div><div><br></div><div>In EC-Engineer you can just configure these HC groups by selecting the SubDevices you want in the group --&gt; right click --&gt; Create group. And then create the HC group. You can also find the description in the manual. </div><div><br></div><div>Best regards,</div><div>Kai Feifel<br><div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
