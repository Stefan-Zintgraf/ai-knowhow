### ARTICLE00_SUBJECT
How to know the DC operating status in real time

### ARTICLE00_BODY
<p> </p><div> <p><span lang="EN-US">Dear Support Team</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">Mitsubishi wants to notify users in real time of DC operating status (1)-(3).</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">How do they obtain this information?</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">(1)Is the master running and working on DC?</span></p>
<p><span> ・<span lang="EN-US">They understand that during cable redundancy, if a disconnection occurs between the main port and the reference clock, DC synchronization stops.</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">However, they do not know how to obtain that status. Please teach them the Notify and Getter that can retrieve the status.</span></span></p>
<p><span> ・<span lang="EN-US">Notify “EC_NOTIFY_DC_STATUS” is described in the manual as being executed at configuration change or initialization.</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">However, they do not know how to distinguish between these.</span></span></p>
<p><span> ・<span lang="EN-US">Notify “EC_NOTIFY_DC_STATUS” is not executed when the reference clock is absent.</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">Does this mean that it remains normal during reference clocking?</span></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">(2)Are the slaves in sync?(When Window Monitoring is enabled)</span></p>
<p><span> ・<span lang="EN-US">Notify “EC_NOTIFY_DC_SLV_SYNC” does not appear to be executed when synchronizing for the first time at startup.</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">Since the execution condition of “EC_NOTIFY_DC_STATUS” is “All devices are synchronized”, does that mean this is executed?</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">However, as mentioned above, this Notify cannot determine “configuration change” and “synchronization normal”.</span></span></p>
<p><span> ・<span lang="EN-US">Notify “EC_NOTYFI_DC_SLV_SYNC” is not executed when the reference clock is absent.</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">Does this mean that it remains normal during reference clocking?</span></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">(3)Is the master performing DCM synchronization</span></p>
<p><span> ・<span lang="EN-US">It is obtained with ecatDcmGetStatus(), but the function returns normal even if it seems that the DC synchronization in (1) has not been performed.</span></span></p>
<p><span> <span lang="EN-US">Do they need to make a decision in OR with (1)?</span></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">Best regards,</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">Haruto Itoyama</span></p>
</div>
