### ARTICLE00_BODY
<div>
<p>Dear Support Team</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I got a question about EC_NOTIFY_FRAME_RESPONSE_ERROR from Ono Sokki.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Their usage environment is as follows.</p>
<p>CPU: Intel Xeon D-1567</p>
<p>OS: VxWorks7 SR650</p>
<p>(Used on the same CPU as Windows10 using Hypervisor)</p>
<p> </p>
<p>There is no problem when running at 1ms,</p>
<p>but if they run at 100us EC_NOTIFY_FRAME_RESPONSE_ERROR seems to occur.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I asked them to review the BIOS settings as it might be the effect of SMI.</p>
<p>However, EC_NOTIFY_FRAME_RESPONSE_ERROR still occurs after the review.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>During their verification, they found the following trends:</p>
<p>If they move the mouse connected via USB during communication,</p>
<p>the processing time of eUsrJob_ProcessAllRxFrames and eUsrJob_SendAllCycFrames will increase,</p>
<p>and it seems that the cycle will not be in time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Q1</p>
<p>Is there a possibility that mouse operation will affect VxWorks?</p>
<p>Is there anything that could be tried for this phenomenon, such as settings?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Q2</p>
<p>In the first place, is it possible to communicate with 100us in an environment</p>
<p>where Windows and VxWorks are running at the same time with Hypervisor?</p>
<p>Do you have a track record of communication?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Best regards,</p>
<p>Kenji Nakamura</p>
<p> </p>
</div>
