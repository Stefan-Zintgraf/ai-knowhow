### ARTICLE03_SUBJECT
Question about EC-Master(YUSHIN) [M069]

### ARTICLE03_BODY
Hi Takuya-san,  <div><br></div><div>The <strong>logical address</strong> is a <strong>virtual address</strong> used to identify a device or object within the <strong>EtherCAT network</strong> at a higher level of communication. Logical addresses are not related to the physical placement or configuration of the device on the network but instead are used to reference devices or objects in a way that is independent of their actual position in the physical network topology.  </div><div><br></div><div>Q: Is it correct to understand that you are referring to the logical address of the ESC register?</div><div>A: No</div><div><br></div><div>Q: 

How does obtaining this information help in the event of a WKC error?</div><div>A: It does not help you to locate the slave in the configuration.</div><div><br></div><div>Best Regards<div data-signature="true" data-signature-id="2">
