<div style="color:#1f497d;">
<div>Dear,</div>
<div>Recently the customer hacontrols development FSoE device and config the eni file, they find a problem it is different with beckhoff twincat.</div>
<div>They get the ixxat fsoe sdk, and porting the demo(ixxat fsoe integrate with ecmaster) to their target platform, But they find a problem when they config the EL1904 &amp; EL2904.</div>
<div>The attachemnt is the eni and twincat project, The demo eni integrate all the fsoe data into 1 PDO variable. The customer used the twincat and generate a eni, it is same as demo eni.</div>
<div>But the EcEngineer's eni seperate the pdo data. please checking the following picture:</div>
<div><img src="/api/v1/ticket_attachment/8393/46689/193372?view=inline" style="max-width:100%;width:1185px;max-height:397px;"></div>
<div> </div>
<div>From our experience, The twincat mode is easy for FSoE app development, it is integrate the FSoE CMD, data and CRC and so on. The FSoE stack could get the all the FSoE data and seperate it in stack. If the customer use the EcEngineer's ENI, they need mapping all the PDO in app.</div>
<div>So, Dose the EcEngineer support to integrate all the PDO in one variable same as twincat?</div>
<div> </div>
<div>We consider that they could use variable alias(the alias length define as 48bits), and get the data via variable name in pdo. But do you have better suggestion to generate a eni same as twincat?</div>
