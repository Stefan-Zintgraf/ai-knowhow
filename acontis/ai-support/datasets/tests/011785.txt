Dear EtherCAT Support<br> <br> Question about EC-Master(YUSHIN) [E163]<br> <br> There are 2 cases the reconnection fails when EtherCATcommunication disconnection occurs.<br> •Reconnection succeeds automatically when the disconnection occurs after Slave 2.<br> In this case, soon after communication line is reconnected, PDO communication starts. We can just re-connection by confirming that “WCKstate” is normal.<br> •When disconnection occurs at “IN” of slave 1, the reconnection fails.<br> In this case, even after communication line is reconnected, PDO communication does NOT start correctly, but it is possible to transfer Slave to “OPERATINAL”.<br> <img src="/api/v1/ticket_attachment/11785/68053/284886?view=inline" style="max-width:100%;"><br> <br> Are EC Master behaviors different for two cases below?<br> •Case when there are slaves which can be communicated.<br> •Case when there are no slaves which can be communicated.<br> <br> Best regards,<br> Takuya Fujikawa
