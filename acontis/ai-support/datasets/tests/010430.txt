<p><span lang="EN-US">Hi</span><span lang="EN-US"> </span><span lang="EN-US">Support</span><span lang="EN-US"> </span><span lang="EN-US">Team</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"> </span><span lang="EN-US"></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">This is from Mitsubishi (commission No. 40627).</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">EC-Engineer V.3.9.11</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">The access rights of CoE objects specified on the ESI and the access rights displayed on the EC-Engineer are different.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">For example, in the case of an ESI (</span><span lang="EN-US">$B!H!I(B</span><span lang="EN-US">&lt;Access WriteRestrictions=""PreOP</span><span lang="EN-US">$B!H!I(B</span><span lang="EN-US">&gt;rw&lt;/Access&gt;</span><span lang="EN-US">$B!F!G(B</span><span lang="EN-US">) in which access rights for PreOP are specified, all access rights in State are displayed the same in EC-Engineer as </span><span lang="EN-US">$B!H(B</span><span lang="EN-US">-- -- - - - ( RW RW RW )</span><span lang="EN-US">$B!I(B</span><span lang="EN-US">. The same access rights are displayed in the EC-Engineer.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">* The same is reproduced in the Simulator.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">The access rights of CoE objects on ESI and the display of access rights in EC-Engineer should be the same, and we think that the access rights of State without specification are RO.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">[Reproduction Procedure]</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">1. Use ESI with the access right specified as rw only for the PreOP state.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">2. Add the Slave from 1. and display the CoE Object-Dictionary.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">3. Swithc to </span><span lang="EN-US">$B!H(B</span><span lang="EN-US">Diagnostic mode</span><span lang="EN-US">$B!I(B</span><span lang="EN-US"> and display </span><span lang="EN-US">$B!H(B</span><span lang="EN-US">Description from ESI</span><span lang="EN-US">$B!I(B</span><span lang="EN-US">.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><p> </p></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">Please refer the attached PDF and ESI file.</span></p>
