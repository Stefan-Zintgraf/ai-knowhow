<div>What are the conditions for using the API used in "Diagnosis Master General"?</div>
<div> </div>
<div>(1) EcDevice.MasterVersion()</div>
<div>What is the condition when the version of MainDevice could not be obtained? What are the conditions under which the displayed value becomes "Unknown"?</div>
<div>(When the value of EcDevice.MasterVersion() could not be obtained)</div>
<div> </div>
<div>(2) EcDevice.MasterInfo.DcInSync()</div>
<div>What are the conditions under which the contents displayed by DC in-sync become Yes/No?</div>
<div>(Conditions for enabling/disabling EcDevice.MasterInfo.DcInSync())</div>
<div> </div>
<div>(3) EcDevice.MasterInfo.TopologyOk()</div>
<div>What are the conditions for the display content of Topology OK to be Yes/No?</div>
<div>(Conditions for enabling/disabling EcDevice.MasterInfo.TopologyOk())</div>
<div> </div>
<div>(4) EcDevice.MasterInfo.LinkConnected()</div>
<div>
<div>What are the conditions under which Link Connected display content becomes Yes/No?</div>
<div>(Conditions for enabling/disabling EcDevice.MasterInfo.LinkConnected())</div>
</div>
<div> </div>
<div>
<div>(5) EcDevice.MasterInfo.SlavesInMasterState()</div>
<div>What are the conditions under which Slaves in Master State display content becomes Yes/No?</div>
<div>(Conditions for enabling/disabling EcDevice.MasterInfo.SlavesInMasterState())</div>
<div> </div>
<div>(6) EcDevice.GetMemoryUsage()</div>
<div>What are the conditions under which the display content of Current [kB] becomes “-”?</div>
<div>(When currentMemory cannot be obtained by EcDevice.GetMemoryUsage())</div>
<div> </div>
<div>(7) EcDevice.GetMemoryUsage()</div>
<div>What are the conditions under which Max [kB] is displayed as Yes/No?</div>
<div>(In case maxMemory cannot be obtained by EcDevice.GetMemoryUsage())</div>
<div> </div>
