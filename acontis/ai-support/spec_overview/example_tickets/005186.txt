### ARTICLE06_BODY
<div>
<p>Hi Andrea,</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I believe the reason everything stops is due to Ecat rules. When invalid data is received the rule is to reject the invalid data and retain the old data. There is a setting ecat[0].AllowInputsOnWkcErr that if you set =1 in the application it will then allow new inputs even in these error conditions.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As for why you are getting errors in the first place, I do not have a good answer but perhaps a test you could try.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>First some background.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In the beginning days of PWM amplifiers, maybe you remember the SemiPower drives. We found that we needed to strip a section of the PWM cable down to the outer braided shield and clamp this to a grounding bar. The cables themselves were just not making a good enough ground contact to dissipate the PWM switching noise. Perhaps you need to do something similar to the Ethercat cables near the drives.</p>

<p>In the beginning days of PWM amplifiers, maybe you remember the SemiPower drives. We found that we needed to strip a section of the PWM cable down to the outer braided shield and clamp this to a grounding bar. The cables themselves were just not making a good enough ground contact to dissipate the PWM switching noise. Perhaps you need to do something similar to the Ethercat cables near the drives.</p>

<p>In the beginning days of PWM amplifiers, maybe you remember the SemiPower drives.
